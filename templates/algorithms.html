Algorithm Technical Details
Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)

Principle: Simulates social behavior of bird flocking

Strengths:

  • Fast convergence
  • Good global search ability
  • Simple implementation

Parameters:

  • Particles: 50
  • Inertia weight: 0.6
  • Cognitive factor: 2.0
  • Social factor: 2.0
Simulated Annealing (SA)

Principle: Mimics the annealing process in metallurgy

Strengths:

  • Avoids local optima
  • Robust convergence
  • Theoretical guarantees

Parameters:

  • Initial temp: 200
  • Cooling rate: 0.98
  • Min temp: 0.001
  • Max iterations: 150
Artificial Fish Swarm Algorithm (AFSA)

Principle: Simulates fish schooling behavior

Strengths:

  • Strong exploration ability
  • Adaptive behavior
  • Parallel processing

Parameters:

  • Fish count: 40
  • Visual range: 0.4
  • Step size: 0.15
  • Crowding factor: 0.7
